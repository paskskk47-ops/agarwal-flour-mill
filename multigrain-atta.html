Custom Multigrain Mixtures for Better Health

In modern lifestyles, standard single-grain flour might not cover all dietary needs. Multigrain flour (Multigrain Atta) combines the health benefits of multiple power grains, creating a rich source of complex carbohydrates, dietary fiber, and plant-based protein.

Unlike store-bought packet multigrain flours, which often contain high amounts of standard cheap wheat and only tiny percentages of other healthy grains, our **custom grinding service** lets you control the exact ratio. You bring the individual grains, and we scale and grind them together in our stone chakkis. This ensures that every roti you eat is genuinely nutritious and custom-made for your lifestyle.

Popular Grains & Ratios We Grind

You can mix and match any of these grains based on your dietary guidelines:

  • Wheat (गेहूं): The base grain, providing natural elasticity and binding. (Usually 60-70%)
  • Gram / Chana (चना): High in plant protein, helps build muscle strength. (10-15%)
  • Barley / Jau (जौ): Rich in soluble fiber, great for digestion and cooling the stomach. (5-10%)
  • Maize / Makka (मक्का): Adds sweetness, vitamins, and a lovely golden color. (5%)
  • Ragi / Finger Millet (रागी): Heavy source of calcium and iron. (5%)
  • Oats (जई): Excellent for heart health and keeping cholesterol levels low. (5%)

Healthy Formulas We Recommend

  • Diabetic-Friendly Mix: High content of Jau (Barley), Chana, and Oats with low wheat ratio to reduce glycemic impact.
  • High-Fiber Mix: Enhanced portion of Jau, Bajra, and Wheat Bran.
  • High-Protein Mix: Higher percentage of roasted Chana (chickpeas) and Soybeans blended with base wheat.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I clean the grains before bringing them?

Please clean and dry each grain category. You can mix them together in a single container or sack before bringing them to our Rajat Path mill, or we can weigh and mix them together for you in our mixing tubs.

Does multigrain flour feel different to knead?

Since it contains grains without gluten (like chana, ragi, and jau), multigrain flour requires slightly more warm water to knead. Let the dough rest for 15-20 minutes before rolling rotis for best results.

What are the grinding charges for multigrain?

Because grinding multiple grains together requires careful sieve adjustments and slower grinding speeds to handle different grain densities, the rate is ₹8 per kg.
