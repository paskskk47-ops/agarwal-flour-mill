Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Common questions about our hygienic flour mill services, custom thickness grinding, and pricing in Mansarovar, Jaipur.

📍 Where is Agarawal Flour Mill located in Mansarovar?

We are located at Shop no. 16, Zone 35, Rajat Path, Mansarovar, Jaipur (302020). We are centrally located and easily accessible from Swarn Path, Kiran Path, VT Road, and Patrakar Colony.

🌾 What are the flour grinding rates per kg?

Our rates are highly affordable: Wheat (गेहूं) grinding is ₹5/kg, Bajra (बाजरा), Maize (मक्का), and Besan (चना) grinding is ₹6/kg, and premium spices like whole turmeric (हल्दी) are ₹15/kg.

🧼 Do you follow separate grinding processes for spices and gluten-free items?

Yes, absolutely. We use dedicated stone chakkis for grains (wheat, bajra, maize) and separate high-performance pulverizers for spices (red chilli, coriander, turmeric) to avoid cross-contamination and guarantee absolute purity.

⏲️ How much time does the grinding take?

For standard quantities of wheat or grains (up to 15 kg), it takes less than 15 minutes. We grind it fresh and warm right before you, so there is no long wait time.
