About Our Wheat Grinding Service

At Agarawal Flour Mill, we understand that wheat flour (atta) is the foundation of every Indian meal. We utilize traditional stone grinders (atta chakki) combined with modern hygienic processes to grind whole wheat grains into the purest flour possible. Unlike packaged flours found in supermarkets, our stone-ground flour retains the natural wheat bran, endosperm, and germ, keeping all fiber, vitamins, and minerals intact.

You can bring your own choice of wheat (such as Sharbati, Lokwan, or MP wheat) and watch it get ground fresh right before you, ensuring absolute purity with no chemicals, preservatives, or blending agents.

Thickness Settings for Every Recipe

Different recipes require different styles of wheat flour. We adjust our chakki plates precisely to match your exact culinary needs:

  • Fine Grinding (बारीक पिसाई): Perfect for soft, fluffy daily Chapatis, Rotis, and Phulkas that stay fresh and soft for hours.
  • Medium Grinding (मध्यम पिसाई): Best for textured Paranthas, Puris, and Naans.
  • Coarse/Rawa Grinding (दरदरा/सूजी पिसाई): Specially suited for traditional Rajasthani Baati, Churma, Ladoo, and dynamic baking recipes.

Why Stone-Ground Atta is Better than Mill Atta

Packaged flour from roller mills is processed at high speeds and temperatures, which burns away the nutritional values and healthy wheat germ oils. Our traditional chakki operates at low speeds and temperatures, which:

  • Retains 100% of dietary fiber, helping digest food naturally.
  • Keeps the vitamins (B-complex) and minerals (iron, magnesium) intact.
  • Preserves the natural nutty flavor and sweet aroma of wheat grains.
  • Contains absolutely zero bleach or artificial additives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you wash or clean the wheat before grinding?

We recommend customers bring pre-cleaned and dried wheat grains. However, we have a dynamic air-cleaning sieve system to filter out any dust particles or small chaff before feeding the grain into the stones.

How much time does it take to grind wheat?

Grinding a standard 10kg sack of wheat takes roughly 10 to 15 minutes depending on the thickness setting you choose.

Do you grind wheat in the same machine as spices?

Absolutely not. We have strictly segregated machines. Spices are ground in heavy pulverizers, and grains like wheat are ground in dedicated stone chakkis to prevent any cross-contamination, ensuring your wheat flour is never spicy or odorous.
