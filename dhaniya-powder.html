Rich, Aromatic & Pure Coriander (Dhaniya) Powder

Coriander powder (Dhaniya) adds structure, thickness, and a citrusy sweet aroma to Indian curries and gravies. However, commercially sold coriander powder is heavily cut with cheap wheat husk, wild grass seeds, and horse dung. In addition, large manufacturers dry-mill at extreme speeds, heating the spice and vaporizing the delicate volatile oils (linalool), leaving the packaged powder flat and aroma-free.

At Agarawal Flour Mill, we process your dried coriander seeds (Sabut Dhaniya) in cold-pulverizing machines. This retains the essential oils and moisture, resulting in highly aromatic, greenish-brown coriander powder that brings a sweet, fresh flavor to your meals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my coriander seeds are dry enough for grinding?

If you press a coriander seed between your fingers and it breaks easily with a popping sound, it is dry. If it bends or feels soft, sun-dry it for a few hours before bringing it to the mill.

Are there separate grinders for coriander?

Yes. We group mild spices (like coriander and cumin) together, ensuring they are not ground in the chilli grinder, which prevents your coriander powder from picking up red chilli heat or color.

How long does coriander powder stay aromatic?

Because we do not strip the volatile oils, our fresh coriander powder maintains a strong sweet aroma for up to 6-8 weeks when stored in an airtight container.
