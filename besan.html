Pure & Genuine Gram Flour (Besan)

Besan is an essential ingredient in Rajasthani and Indian kitchens, used for making Kadhi, Pakoras, Dhoklas, Laddoos, and the classic Gatte ki Sabji. However, commercial packaged besan is often adulterated with cheap yellow pea flour (Matar Atta) and chemicals, which changes both the flavor and digestive profile of your dishes.

At Agarawal Flour Mill, we grind pure dry Chana Dal (split Bengal gram) right in front of you. This guarantees 100% pure besan with its signature rich yellow color, nutty aroma, and distinct culinary property that commercial alternatives can never replicate.

Fine vs. Coarse Grinding Options

Different recipes call for different besan textures, and our stone grinder can be adjusted instantly:

  • Super Fine Besan (बारीक बेसन): Perfect for smooth batters used in making Kadhi, spongy Khaman Dhoklas, thin cheelas, and crispy evening Pakoras.
  • Coarse Besan / Motia (दरदरा बेसन): Essential for traditional sweets like Besan Ke Ladoo, Mohanthal, Halwa, and savory Rajasthani Gatte.

Health Benefits of Clean Besan

  • High Protein Content: Chickpea flour has higher protein and healthy fiber than regular wheat flour.
  • Gluten-Free: An excellent substitute for wheat-sensitive diets.
  • Low Glycemic Index: Digests slowly, providing stable energy levels and healthy blood sugar regulation.
  • Zero Chemicals: No artificial coloring agents or chemical preservatives added.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring whole black chana or just split chana dal?

We accept both. Split chana dal is preferred for clean yellow besan, while whole brown/black chickpeas (Kala Chana) can be ground to make nutritious dark gram flour.

Do you ensure that wheat particles don't mix with my Besan?

Yes. Since many customers grind besan for gluten sensitivity, we brush and blow-clean the grinding mill thoroughly before running a dal batch to avoid cross-contamination.

How do I keep my Besan fresh?

Keep your fresh besan in a clean, airtight glass or steel jar. Use it within 4-6 weeks for best flavor. Cooking with fresh besan yields a much richer aroma than stale packages.
