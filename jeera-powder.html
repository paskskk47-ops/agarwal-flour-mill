Aromatic & Pure Cumin (Jeera) Powder

Cumin powder (Jeera) is the back-bone of Indian spice blends, famed for its warm, slightly bitter taste and outstanding digestive qualities. Unfortunately, packaged cumin powder in stores is often combined with ground grass seeds, wood shavings, and mineral dyes. Furthermore, standard high-temperature factories dry out the natural cumin oils, leading to a dull grey powder that has lost its authentic flavor.

At Agarawal Flour Mill, we grind whole cumin seeds at low speeds. This preserves the natural oils (cuminaldehyde) that give jeera its warming flavor, leaving you with genuine, highly fragrant cumin powder that will transform the taste of your dry vegetables, curries, and raitas.

Health & Digestion Benefits of Fresh Jeera

  • Boosts Digestion: Cumin stimulates pancreatic enzymes, aiding overall digestion and preventing gut bloating.
  • Rich in Iron: Whole cumin seeds are naturally high in iron, supporting body vitality.
  • Pure Essential Oils: We do not filter or extract volatile oils, giving you the natural nutritional and flavoring benefits of pure cumin.

Grinding Fresh or Roasted Cumin

You can bring raw cumin seeds, or slightly roast them at home before bringing them to our Rajat Path mill. Ground roasted cumin (Bhuna Jeera) is highly popular for sprinkling over curd, Lassi, buttermilk, and salads, giving them a distinct smoky flavor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get bhuna jeera (roasted cumin) ground at the mill?

Yes. Simply roast the seeds at home to release their oils, let them cool down, and bring them in. We will grind them into a rich, aromatic dark brown roasted jeera powder.

Is cumin ground in the chilli pulverizer?

No. We ensure that mild spices like cumin are ground in dedicated chambers that do not handle chillies, ensuring your jeera powder retains its pure flavor without any red chilli heat.

What is the minimum grinding amount for Cumin?

We accept cumin quantities as small as 500g or 1kg. Grinding in small quantities is recommended so that you can enjoy maximum freshness in your kitchen.
