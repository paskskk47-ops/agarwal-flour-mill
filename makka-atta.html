Fresh & Nutritious Makki ka Atta

Maize (Makka) flour is widely loved across North India for its incredible flavor and nutrition, especially when paired with Sarson ka Saag or winter curries. Raw maize kernels are exceptionally hard, requiring powerful stone grinding to break them down into soft, workable flour without burning the nutrients.

At Agarawal Flour Mill on Rajat Path, Mansarovar, we clean your maize kernels to remove any chaff or sand before grinding. Our chakkis are configured to grind yellow or white maize into uniform flour that holds moisture well, making it easy to knead and pat into perfect rotis.

Nutritional Profiles of Freshly Ground Maize

Unlike store-bought yellow cornmeal which is often heavily processed and stripped of its bran, our whole-grain grinding retains the full goodness of the maize:

  • Rich in Beta-Carotene: Yellow maize provides vitamin A, vital for healthy eyes and vision.
  • High Dietary Fiber: Promotes digestive health and keeps you full for longer.
  • Natural Gluten-Free Structure: Safe alternative for those who wish to avoid gluten or rotate grains.
  • B-Complex Vitamins: Essential for energy production and active metabolism.

Custom Grinding Ratios

You can bring your maize as is, or mix it with a small portion of wheat or gram (chana) to increase the binding elasticity of the dough. We will gladly customize the grinding settings according to your preference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prevent my Makki rotis from breaking?

Freshly ground maize flour naturally binds much better than old packaged flour. Knead the dough with warm water, and use a small amount of fresh besan or wheat flour if you prefer extra binding.

Do you grind yellow and white maize separately?

Yes. We ensure the stones are brushed clean before starting any batch so that different colors or varieties of maize do not get mixed up.

How long does maize flour stay fresh?

Since we add no chemical shelf-stabilizers, we suggest storing our fresh maize flour in an airtight steel bin and consuming it within a month.
