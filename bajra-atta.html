Traditional Stone-Ground Bajra Atta

Bajra (Pearl Millet) is a celebrated grain in Rajasthan, especially prized during winters for its warming properties, high energy content, and earthy taste. At Agarawal Flour Mill, we grind whole Bajra seeds using traditional low-speed chakkis. This ensures that the essential fibers and vitamins are not stripped away by excessive heat, providing you with high-fiber, highly cohesive flour.

Whether you use it for making classic Bajre ki Roti, Khichdi, or Rajasthani Rabdi, our fresh stone-ground flour delivers the most authentic taste and nutritional benefits straight to your dining table.

Health Benefits of Fresh Pearl Millet

Buying pre-packaged millet flour can be disappointing, as millets can turn slightly bitter when kept on warehouse shelves for months. Our fresh grinding process guarantees:

  • 100% Gluten-Free: Perfect dietary choice for people with gluten sensitivities or wheat allergies.
  • Rich in Iron & Fiber: Helps improve digestion, regulates blood sugar levels, and fights fatigue.
  • Sweet & Fresh Taste: Absolutely no bitterness, making the dough highly workable and the rotis incredibly flavorful.
  • Essential Proteins: Ground at low temperatures to conserve the delicate proteins naturally present in millets.

Grinding Thickness Preferences

  • Medium-Fine (सामान्य पिसाई): Highly recommended for making soft rotis and paranthas.
  • Coarse (दरदरा पिसाई): Ideal for cooking traditional Bajre ki Khichdi or winter porridge/rabdi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does store-bought bajra flour sometimes taste bitter?

Bajra contains natural oils that oxidize and turn rancid quickly when exposed to air over long shelf periods. Grinding small batches fresh at our Rajat Path mill ensures zero oxidation and a sweet, natural taste.

What is the minimum grinding amount for Bajra?

We grind quantities as small as 1kg. Bring whatever amount you need for your weekly or monthly requirements to enjoy maximum freshness.

How do I store fresh Bajra Atta?

Because our flour contains no artificial preservatives, we recommend storing it in an airtight container in a dry, cool place and consuming it within 3-4 weeks for best taste and nutrition.
