Custom Blending of Whole Spices

Garam Masala is the crown jewel of Indian cooking, giving dishes their rich, warming complexity. Every household has its own secret family recipe, using different ratios of whole spices like cinnamon (dalchini), cardamom (elaichi), cloves (laung), black pepper (kali mirch), bay leaves (tej patta), cumin (jeera), coriander (dhaniya), nutmeg (jaiphal), and mace (javitri).

Pre-packaged store garam masala is usually flat, stale, and packed with cheap fillers like heavy coriander husk. At Agarawal Flour Mill on Rajat Path, Mansarovar, you bring your own custom recipe of raw whole spices. We mix and pulverize them together in our dedicated spice grinder at controlled low temperatures. This ensures the delicate volatile oils do not heat up and escape, providing you with fresh, intensely aromatic garam masala that lasts longer and tastes richer.

Common Ingredients in a Premium Blend

Here is a list of whole spices you can dry, mix, and bring for custom grinding:

  • Black Pepper & Cloves: Add heat, sharpness, and preservative qualities.
  • Cinnamon & Green Cardamom: Provide a sweet, woodsy, and citrusy undertone.
  • Black Cardamom & Cumin: Give a smoky, earthy, and deep base layer.
  • Nutmeg, Mace & Star Anise: Provide strong sweet-spiced aromas (use in small quantities).
  • Bay Leaves (Tej Patta): Add herbal freshness and bind the dry ingredients.

Our Grinding Method

Whole spices have vastly different shapes, oil levels, and hardness. Cardamom has a fibrous husk, while nutmeg is extremely dense. We use specialized hammer-mill pulverizers that reduce all these ingredients into a uniform, fine, and aromatic powder. The grinder is thoroughly cleaned beforehand to guarantee the absolute purity of your family's masala recipe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to roast the spices before bringing them?

Slightly dry-roasting the spices on a tawa helps release their natural oils and moisture, which leads to a much finer powder and a richer, toasted aroma. Let them cool down completely before bringing them to the mill.

What are the grinding charges for custom spice mixtures?

Because whole spice mixtures contain oily and fibrous materials that must be ground carefully at slow speeds, the rate is ₹20 per kg.

How do I keep my custom Garam Masala fresh?

Garam Masala is highly sensitive to air. Keep it in an airtight glass container, away from direct sunlight, and always close the lid tightly. Use a dry spoon for scooping.
