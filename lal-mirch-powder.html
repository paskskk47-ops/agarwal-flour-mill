Genuine Red Chilli Powder Grinding

Red Chilli powder (Lal Mirch) defines the color and pungency of Indian cuisine. Unfortunately, commercial pre-packaged chilli powder is frequently adulterated with brick dust, toxic red oil dyes, and low-grade seed waste. Many commercial manufacturers also extract capsaicin (chilli oil extract) before packaging, leading to dull, flat spice powders that lack natural depth.

At Agarawal Flour Mill on Rajat Path, Mansarovar, you can bring whole dried chillies (like Rajasthani Mathania, Kashmiri, or Teja chillies) and watch them get pulverized. We handle your chillies with extreme care, ensuring a clean process that delivers rich, red, and aromatic red chilli powder that is completely free of chemical dyes and fillers.

Heat Levels Tailored for You

Depending on the style of chillies you bring and your grinding instructions, we can customize the spice texture:

  • Fiery Hot Powder (बारीक तीखी मिर्च): Finely ground whole chillies (seeds included) to add authentic Rajasthani heat to your curries.
  • Medium-Spiced Red Powder (मध्यम मिर्च): Mildly ground for everyday household cooking.
  • Coarse Chilli Flakes / Kutti Mirch (कुटी हुई मिर्च): Coarsely crushed red chillies, perfect for pickles, street food toppings, and dynamic seasoning.

Our Safe & Clean Grinding Environment

Chilli particles can easily irritate the eyes and nose. We use enclosed, high-suction spice pulverizers that contain all spice dust inside the machines. This protects the environment of the mill and ensures your red chilli is packed safely. Furthermore, we brush down the machine thoroughly after each run to ensure no residual chillies contaminate other mild spices like coriander or cumin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I sun-dry the chillies before bringing them to the mill?

Yes. Genuinely crisp, sun-dried chillies yield a much finer powder and prevent the machines from choking. Please remove the green stalks (dandi) at home for the purest red color.

Is Kashmiri Mirch ground at the same rate?

Yes. Any dried whole red chilli variety is ground at our standard rate of ₹12/kg. Kashmiri chillies will yield a bright crimson, low-heat powder, whereas Teja or local Rajasthani chillies will be fiery hot.

How do I store fresh red chilli powder?

Store it in an airtight jar in a cool, dark cabinet. Avoid using wet spoons to maintain its rich color and prevent spoilage.
